Privacy Policy for BLOCK 100

Last updated: September 30, 2026

JS K&Y Fam Studio ("we", "us", "our") operates the mobile game BLOCK 100 (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our App.

Information We Collect

BLOCK 100 does not require you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information.

We collect the following limited information:

  1. Anonymous gameplay analytics. We collect anonymous, non-identifying events about how the App is used (for example: a stage was started, a stage was cleared or failed, a rewarded ad retry was offered or used, an in-app purchase flow was started or completed). These events never include your name, email, device ID, advertiser ID, or any other identifier — only gameplay facts such as stage number, score, and elapsed time. This data is used solely to improve game balance and difficulty.
  2. Advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID / AAID). To show ads (rewarded video ads and, in limited cases, interstitial ads) through Google AdMob, AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier and other technical data as described in Google's Privacy & Terms and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device's system settings.
  3. Purchase information. If you purchase "Remove Ads Forever," the purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not see or store your payment card details. We only receive confirmation from Google Play that the purchase was completed, which we use to remove ads and unlock the ad-free experience on your device.

Children's Privacy

BLOCK 100 is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the email below.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share limited technical data (such as the advertising identifier) with our service providers strictly to operate the App:

  • Google AdMob — to display ads and process rewarded-ad rewards.
  • Google Play Billing — to process the "Remove Ads Forever" purchase.
  • Google Analytics / Firebase (if enabled in a future update) — to collect the anonymous gameplay analytics described above.

These providers may process data outside your country of residence, in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Data Retention & Deletion

Because BLOCK 100 does not use accounts or store personal information on our servers, there is no personal profile to delete. Gameplay progress (stage progress, score, settings) is stored locally on your device and is removed when you uninstall the App. To reset your advertising identifier or limit ad tracking, use your device's system settings.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
